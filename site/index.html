A bridge, not a destination

A finite route through your reading.

Feed Later Bridge handles the handoff. The article still belongs to its publisher, and the queue still belongs to you.

  1. 01

    Connect the feed

    Paste the private or public RSS/Atom URL your reader gives you. The extension asks only for that origin.

  2. 02

    Work the queue

    Duplicate links merge automatically. Search, annotate, and mark items finished without creating an account.

  3. 03

    Take it back out

    Export the complete queue as readable Markdown, interoperable OPML, or structured JSON at any time.

Install in two minutes

Keep the bridge in your browser.

Version 1 ships as a reviewable Chrome-compatible package. Firefox packaging is a planned next step.

  1. 1

    Download the extension zip and unpack it.

  2. 2

    Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.

  3. 3

    Choose “Load unpacked” and select the extracted folder.
