How it works

Use your saved-items feed in three steps

Feed Later Bridge keeps a small queue. It imports links and metadata; it does not fetch article pages.

  1. 01

    Add a feed URL

    Paste the RSS or Atom saved-items URL from your reader.

  2. 02

    Manage your queue

    Search items, add notes, finish reading, or undo a removal.

  3. 03

    Export your queue

    Download Markdown, OPML, or JSON whenever you need it.

Install the extension

Import your own saved-items feed

Download the Chrome-compatible package, then load it from Chrome’s extensions page.

  1. 1

    Download the extension zip and unpack it.

  2. 2

    Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

  3. 3

    Choose “Load unpacked” and select the extracted folder.
