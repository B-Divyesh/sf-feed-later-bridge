Privacy

Your queue data stays in your browser unless you export a file.

Data the extension stores

The extension stores the feed URL, article titles, links, notes, and status in local extension storage. This data is not sent to this project.

Feed requests

When you import, your browser connects directly to the exact feed origin you entered. Requests omit browser credentials and redirects are blocked. Use the final direct feed URL if an import reports a redirect.

Website data

This website uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts. Static hosting may retain routine security logs under its own policy.

Deletion and export

Remove entries in the queue or uninstall the extension to delete local storage. Export Markdown, OPML, or JSON before deletion if you need a copy.

Contact

Questions and security reports belong in the public issue tracker (opens GitHub). Do not include private feed URLs in an issue.
