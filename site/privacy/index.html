Privacy

Feed Later Bridge is designed so your reading list does not need to leave your browser.

Data the extension stores

The extension stores the feed URL you provide, imported article titles and links, source metadata, notes, and completion status in your browser’s local extension storage. This data is not sent to us.

Feed requests

When you choose “Import feed,” your browser connects directly to the exact feed origin you entered. Feed requests omit browser credentials, block cross-origin redirects, and happen only when you initiate an import. The feed provider can observe that network request under its own policy.

Website data

This website uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts. Static hosting may retain routine security logs such as IP address and request time; the project does not receive a user profile from them.

Deletion and export

You can remove individual entries in the queue and uninstall the extension to delete its local storage. You can export your full queue as Markdown, OPML, or JSON before doing so.

Contact

Questions and security reports can be opened in the project’s public issue tracker. Do not include private feed URLs in an issue.
